System Overview & Data Workflow

Objective

The system aims to provide early, reliable, and region-specific warnings of heatwave conditions, enabling timely response by meteorologists, disaster-management officers, and the general public[cite: 4].

"Timely and accurate heatwave intelligence saves lives. This system bridges the gap between raw weather data and actionable public advisories."[cite: 4]
— Chief Meteorologist, Disaster Management Cell[cite: 4]

Severe Heatwave Alert Bulletin

Alert: Severe Heatwave Warning issued for Region: Vidarbha & Marathwada Zone[cite: 5]

Maximum recorded temperature: 47°C, exceeding normal by 6°C[cite: 5]. Bulletin Version: 2.1, issued by AWS network in coordination with the IMD[cite: 5]. Citizens are advised to avoid outdoor exposure between 12 PM and 4 PM[cite: 5].

Forecast & Alert Workflow

  2. Analyse heatwave conditions
